# Mendonça L. et al. (2026)

In industrial environments, the effective maintenance planning depends not only on accurate predictions but also on the interpretability and adaptability of decision-support systems. However, most Predictive Maintenance (PdM) models operate as black boxes, limiting the human trust and preventing the dynamic collaboration between humans and machines. To address these limitations, this paper proposes a Digital Twin-based architecture that integrates predictive, explainable, and agentic intelligence for the transparent and human-centred maintenance decision-making. The architecture is aligned with the ISO 23247 standard, ensuring the interoperability and structured data exchange across physical and virtual layers. It incorporates an Explainability Component that employs Explainable AI techniques and recommendation mechanisms to translate model outputs into actionable insights, and an Agentic AI module to orchestrate reasoning, manage human-AI dialogue, and enable continuous learning based on operator feedback. The proposed architecture was conceptually validated through an analytical assessment of its architecture and components, confirming its feasibility and potential for industrial implementation. By closing the loop between prediction, explanation, and human interaction, it advances PdM toward adaptive, transparent, and human-centred systems consistent with Industry 5.0 principles.  © 2026 IEEE.
