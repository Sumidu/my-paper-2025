# Khun J.L. (2026)

This chapter examines how agentic AI and robotics reshape autonomous vehicles and human-AI interaction, integrating technical, ethical, regulatory, and human centered perspectives to accelerate responsible adoption. It contrasts reactive, deliberative, and hybrid architectures, outlines perception, sensor fusion, and control challenges, and highlights bias, explainability, privacy, and emotional intelligence limits that affect trust. The chapter argues that human factors, transparent interfaces, and progressive disclosure are essential to acceptance, and that pilots, open data, standardized KPIs, and governance frameworks (audit trails, accountability, cross sector boards) enable scaling. It assesses mobility, traffic, safety, environmental impacts, and socioeconomic shifts, and proposes incentive, workforce transition, and evaluation strategies to ensure equitable, resilient deployment of AVs and agentic systems. © 2026 by IGI Global Scientific Publishing.
